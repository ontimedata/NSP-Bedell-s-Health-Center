What We Offer

Natural Wellness Services

Each session is offered as gentle education and support, thoughtfully personalized to you.

Health Nutritional Counseling

Personalized, food-first guidance to help you understand how everyday nourishment supports your overall wellbeing. Together we explore gentle, sustainable habits tailored to your lifestyle, always as education and encouragement, never a prescription.

Biomagnetism

A natural, non-invasive approach that uses the gentle placement of magnets to encourage the body's own sense of balance and harmony. Sessions are calm and restful, offered as a complementary way to support your wellness journey.

Holistic Iridology

A gentle, observational practice that studies patterns in the iris as a window into your constitutional strengths and tendencies. It is used purely as an educational tool to guide personalized wellness conversations, never as a diagnosis.

Aromatherapy

Thoughtfully selected botanical essential oils chosen to create moments of calm, comfort, and sensory balance. We will explore aromas and blends that help you feel grounded and supported throughout daily life.

Muscle Testing

A gentle, hands-on feedback method used to better understand how your body responds, helping guide individualized wellness choices. It is a supportive, educational tool that informs the personalized care we create together.
