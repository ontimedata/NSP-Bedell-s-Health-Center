Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 1, 2026 · Last updated: July 1, 2026

NSP Bedell's Health Center ("we," "us," or "our"), the holistic wellness practice of Alejandrina Bedell, respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through our website (the "Site") and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

  • Contact & booking details — your name, email, phone number, and the details you share when you request a consultation or send a message (our booking tool is powered by GoHighLevel, a product of HighLevel, Inc.).
  • Wellness information you choose to share — health goals, concerns, and lifestyle details you provide so we can offer wellness guidance. You decide what to share with us.
  • Usage & device data — IP address, browser and device type, and pages viewed, collected automatically by our hosting provider and cookies.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Respond to inquiries and schedule and conduct consultations;
  • Provide wellness guidance and product recommendations you request;
  • Operate, secure, and improve the Site;
  • Send appointment reminders and, with your consent, wellness updates;
  • Comply with legal obligations.

3. Handling of Health-Related Information

Any health or wellness information you share is treated with care and used only to serve you. We do not sell it. Please note that our wellness services are not medical care, and this Site is not a patient portal — please do not submit information you consider highly sensitive through website forms or email.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us operate — such as GoHighLevel / HighLevel, Inc. (booking/CRM) and our website hosting provider — and where required by law or to protect rights and safety.

5. Data Retention & Security

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law, and we use reasonable safeguards to protect it. No system is completely secure.

6. Your California Privacy Rights

California residents may request access to, deletion of, or correction of their personal information, and may opt out of any "sale" or "sharing" (we do not sell or share it). We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights. Contact us using the details below to make a request.

7. Children's Privacy

The Site is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from children. Services for minors are arranged with a parent or guardian.

8. Third-Party Links

The Site may link to third-party websites and platforms. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy; changes are posted here with a new "Last updated" date.
